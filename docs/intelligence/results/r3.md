## Verifier fixes

1. **A consumed item came back on the next visit's first page (4b.5, real bug).**
   - The harness, once it really consumed items (fix 3), found it under `home@2c-r15`: a viewer whose unseen connection items all belonged to one friend got 2 of the 2 items he had consumed 45 minutes earlier back on page 1. Their `score'` was 0.65 (m = 0.154), while 40 unseen pool items scored about 4.1 just below them.
   - Cause: the diversity re-ranker took the best connection item that keeps the rules. The 2-per-author cap blocked the friend's unseen items, so the seen items of other friends filled the slots. The R1 `relief` hook only fires when *no* connection item keeps the rules.
   - Fix: a `contest(position, page, picked)` hook in `diversity.rerank` (`intel/ranking/diversity.py`), used by `home._mix_diverse`. When the pick is an item the exposure pass pushed below `revisit.min_m`, the best pool item that keeps every rule and scores higher after the multiplier takes the slot; the seen item waits for a later slot. It counts toward the share (a floor, as for `relief`) and runs only in the continuing arms. The control (`home@2c`, no continuation) is unchanged (decision 23).
   - Result under r15: consumed items back in B's page 1 went from max 2 to **0**. Page-1 Jaccard max went from 0.38 to **0**. Scenario C first pages went from 31% to **26%**.
   - Tests: `test_diversity.py::test_a_seen_connection_item_yields_its_slot_to_a_better_pool_item`. It fails without the fix: seen items are on page 1. `test_the_contest_never_runs_in_the_control_arm` checks the control's order is the same.
2. **First-page p95 under `home@2c-r15` was 194-207 ms against 150 (A8 open item).**
   - A profile of 180 first pages showed 55% of the time in `diversity.rerank`, which evaluated each constraint method for every window item at every slot.
   - Fix: `_Page.predicate(names)` reads the page state once per slot (the blocked authors and format) and answers with a set lookup. `relief` and `contest` use it too.
   - The per-candidate rule E7 no longer deep-copies the `integrity` section (`intel/eligibility.py`).
   - `test_the_page_predicate_answers_like_the_ok_methods` checks the same answers as the `ok_*` methods for 6 seeds, 4 run/gap settings and every relaxation level. The parity, golden and property suites are green.
   - Result: **p95 115 ms** (r15) and **100 ms** (control).
3. **The harness reported "consumed items of A in B" as a constant 0.**
   - `_simulate` appended a literal `0` ("a 4 s dwell is viewed, not consumed"), so the 09 criterion was never measured.
   - Fix: a new `--consume N` option (default 2). The first N of the dwelled items get state `consumed`, as a like would, and the harness counts them in B's page 1 at +45 min and at +6 h.
   - The residual-row check also counted visit rows that real dev use had written for a chosen viewer (dev now has real `intel_feed_session` rows), so every run aborted. It now counts only rows started since the run began.
4. **`comment_state` had only its primary key (track A open item 4).** Three first-page reads scanned it: the commenter feature, the conversation read and the conversation exception.
   - Fix: migration `feed/0013_comment_state_indexes`, built `CONCURRENTLY` (`atomic = False`) like `0012`, on `(post_id, occurred_at)`, `(author_id, occurred_at)` and `(post_author_id, occurred_at)`. The indexes are also declared in the model.
   - Tests: `test_feed_index_migration.py` (2 new). Applied on dev.
5. **09 said a consumed item reaches m ≥ 0.9 "at +11 days".** 06 §5.3 computes 0.874 there, and 07 WP 4b.5 uses +12.5 d after one exposure and +16.5 d after two. 09 is corrected to 07's numbers; the unit tests already asserted them.

## Deviations

1. **Control arm (`home@2c`): one viewer gets 2 consumed items back.** About 85% of that viewer's unseen connection inventory is one author's. Without pool items (decision 23 keeps the control at 0% outside thin inventory), page 1 cannot hold both R1's "≤ 2 per author per page" and "≤ 1 consumed item back". The author cap wins. The recommending arms meet both.
2. **Scenario C is an early run (09 R3 Tests) and is not met.** With 06's `displayed` curve (m0 0.55, h 12 h), an item shown 45 minutes ago keeps m ≈ 0.57. When the viewer's unseen items score less than 0.57 × the seen ones (flat score distributions), the seen ones come back. That is rotation by `score · m` as specified, not a bug. The suite is R6.
3. The track deviations stand as recorded in the track notes: [r3-track-a.md](r3-track-a.md) §Deviations 2-12 and [r3-track-b.md](r3-track-b.md) §Deviations. Track A's deviation 1 (09's "+11 days") is now fixed.

## Open items

1. **Owner or tuning decision: the `displayed` curve.**
   - Measured: with the r15 arm, a steeper `exposure.m0.displayed` 0.2 and `half_life_h.displayed` 8.67 bring scenario C from 25.8% to 14.8% and still meet the revisit numbers.
   - Adopting them needs a new draft version, because stored drafts are immutable.
   - Decision 25 ("For You" by default once 4b passes) should wait for this. All the other R3 exit numbers hold under r15.
2. **CLS on a slow network: 2.7 to 3.3 against 0.1.**
   - The shifts come from cards that grow after they are drawn: the snapshot-to-live swap, the comment preview a `PostCard` loads on its own, and media boxes without a reserved size. The next-page skeleton moves with them.
   - The fix belongs in `PostCard` and `FeedCardSkeleton` (reserve the media box from the snapshot's dimensions; render the comment preview in a fixed slot). The spec measures CLS without failing on it (`E2E_CLS_DEBUG=1` prints each shift).
3. **Next pages carry no `visit_ord` in their serve tokens.** It needs the ordinal in the ranked cursor and in the keyset cursor.
4. **The `personalization_off` switch (R4, WP 8.x) must also purge** `feed:sess`, `feed:exp` and `feed:head`, plus the viewer's `intel_feed_session` rows. Today there is no API that sets the flag. A hash written before the flag was set would keep receiving `last_activity_at` touches from later pages, and phase-2 server-side bus rows are not gated (track B deviation 10).
5. **Consumption gaps:**
   - "Watch ≥ 30 s on a long video counts as consumed" has no config key yet.
   - Bus likes, comments, shares and saves reach the ledger only through the aggregator (≤ 60 s), not the hot ledger.
   - The aggregator's `visits` counts distinct client sessions, an approximation of distinct visits.
6. **The throttle of 30 first pages a minute per account** can make back-to-back e2e runs on the shared account hit a 429. `home-refresh` waits for the quota; run the specs with `--workers=1`.
