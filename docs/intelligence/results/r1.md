## What the verifier found and fixed

1. **The author cap broke when pool alternatives existed (4b.10, real bug).** In the recommending arms (`home@2b-r15`, `-r30`), a viewer whose connection inventory belongs mostly to one author got up to 17 posts by that author on one page. Pool items from other authors were waiting in the continuation tail, but the re-ranker relaxed the cap instead of using them. Before the fix, the harness found 3 such pages under r15 with alternatives, and a direct probe over 400 recipients found 32. The fix is a `relief(position, page, names)` hook in `diversity.rerank`. It is asked at every relaxation level before a constraint is dropped. In a variant that continues into pools, it gives the slot to a pool item that keeps those constraints, and the connection item moves to a later page. The control (`home@2b`, no continuation) gets no relief and keeps its decision-23 cap of 10%. After the fix: 0 pages over the cap under r15 and r30, in both selections; the probe also found 0. New tests: `test_continuation.py::test_r15_keeps_the_author_cap_with_pool_alternatives` (fails on the old code: 4 posts by one friend on page 1) and `test_control_keeps_its_cap_and_does_not_fill_with_pools`.
2. **The "why" lines overclaimed.** «منشور عليه تفاعل كبير دلوقتي» / "Popular right now" was shown for a post that may have a single reaction (`pools.popular.min_engagement` is 1). "A new post you may like" implied personalization, but the pools are not personalized. The new lines are «منشور عام الناس بتتفاعل معاه» / "A public post people are engaging with" and «منشور عام جديد» / "A new public post".
3. **The Arabic count floor was not bidi-safe.** "+20 منشور" in an RTL line can render with the sign on either side. The line is now «20 منشور أو أكثر في آخر 24 ساعة». The floor rule and the named window are unchanged. The vitest and e2e expectations were updated.
4. **A flaky test.** `test_seed_command_scale_and_replace` seeded the command's RNG with a random account id, so its row counts were a random draw. It now uses a fixed id, and both full runs passed.
5. **redis-hot was configured live, not from compose.** The running dev container still had `--maxmemory 128mb` in its command; 384 MB had been set with `CONFIG SET`. It was recreated from compose; it keeps no data on disk. It now runs `--maxmemory 384mb` with a 448 MB limit.

No unauthorized content, private-behaviour leak, location data, fabricated count, literal ranking threshold or event outside a transaction was found. Pool items pass every DISCOVERY rule again at serve time, with the viewer's claims, and later pages re-check E9b.

## Deviations

- **Mix share in the arms:** the share is a floor, not an exact value. When relief fills slots the cap closes, the page-1 share rises above 0.15 for viewers whose connection inventory is one author's. The mean over 20 viewers is 0.19, inside 0.15 ± 0.05. Track A's "exactly 0.15 for every user" was measured before the cap fix.
- **Track A's deviations stand:** recommended slots are claimed inside the re-ranker; continuation follows the variant; pool items use source `discovery`; the data migration writes no outbox event.
- **Track B's deviations stand:**
  - the reels cursor carries a snapshot time;
  - a bad reels or video cursor is a 400 (it used to be a 404);
  - followers-only videos are bounded to the 50 most recent such authors.
